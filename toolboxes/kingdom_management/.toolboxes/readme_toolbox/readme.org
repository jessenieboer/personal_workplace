*** Details
**** coneceptual organization:
Each kingdom and region has a person or group that reigns over it.
Kingdoms can contain regions, goals, tasks, projects and collections.
Tasks and projects are aimed at goals.
Tasks begin with verbs; goals are noun phrases.
Projects can contain tasks, but tasks can be independent of projects.
Collections can contain files, documents, projects, and/or objects.
**** practical organization
A kingdom is represented by a top-level container with a hierarchy of sub-containers for its regions.

The kingdom structure exists in a project management tool and is mirrored in a local directory structure. Source of truth is the tool; local directory structure can have extra stuff in it.

Project content and collections exist in local directories. Projects are the connection between tool and local.
